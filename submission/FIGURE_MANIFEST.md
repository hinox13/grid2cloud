# Figure manifest

draws it and its hash. Generated by `submission/make_manifest.py`; machine-readable copy in
`manifest.json`. Value-level sources for every annotation are in [`FIGURE_SOURCES.md`](FIGURE_SOURCES.md),
and every number quoted in the text is re-checked by `submission/check_post.py`.
